    PART 3: AI Prompt Pack — Write Any Freelance Document in 5 Minutes


    Copy these into ChatGPT exactly as written. Replace [BRACKETS] with your specific info.


    Prompt 1 — Write a cold email

    "Write a cold email from a freelance [social media manager / copywriter / web designer] to a [restaurant / coaching business / startup] in India. The email should: mention a specific thing about their business that I can improve, offer a free sample of work, be under 150 words, have a casual friendly tone, and end with a low-pressure call to action. Business name: [NAME]. One specific issue I noticed: [ISSUE]."


    Prompt 2 — Write a proposal

    "Write a freelance proposal for a [social media content] project. The client is a [describe their business]. The project scope is: [describe what they asked for]. My proposed price is Rs[X]. Include: project overview, what I'll deliver, timeline, price, and a brief note about my process. Keep it under 400 words and make it sound professional but warm."


    Prompt 3 — Reply to a client inquiry

    "Write a reply to this client message: '[PASTE THEIR MESSAGE]'. I am a freelance [writer/designer]. My reply should: address their specific question, briefly describe my relevant experience, suggest a next step (call or free sample), and be under 200 words."


    Prompt 4 — Write a LinkedIn post to attract clients

    "Write a LinkedIn post from a freelance [social media manager] targeting Indian small business owners. The post should share ONE specific insight or tip about [Instagram marketing for local businesses]. It should be 150-200 words, end with a question to drive comments, and have a soft call to action to DM for services. Tone: helpful expert, not salesy."


    Prompt 5 — Follow up on unanswered email

    "Write a short follow-up email to a prospect who didn't reply to my initial pitch 5 days ago. My original email offered [free sample social media posts] for their [restaurant]. The follow-up should: reference that I sent something before, add a new reason to respond (e.g. I'm only taking 2 more clients this month), and be under 100 words."

    PART 5: Where to Find Your First Client (This Week)


    Day 1-2: Local businesses

  • Search Google Maps for restaurants, boutiques, gyms, salons in your city
  • Check which ones have bad/inactive Instagram accounts
  • Find their contact email via website or Google "business name email"
  • Send Template 1 above

  • Create profile on: Fiverr, Contra.com, PeoplePerHour

  • Post Template 4 (LinkedIn post prompt) on your profile

  • Check Fiverr inbox for inquiries
  • Reply to any comments on your LinkedIn post
